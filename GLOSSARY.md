# SprayWise

SprayWise analyzes weather observations at multiple heights in an orchard to support an applicator's decisions.

## Language

**Observation height**:
The vertical distance of a weather measurement above the local ground surface. It is distinct from elevation above sea level.
_Avoid_: Elevation when referring to sensor height above ground.

**Multi-height observation**:
A set of weather readings associated with different observation heights at a site and comparable observation times.

**Observed wind profile**:
Wind speeds and directions measured at the available observation heights. It describes those sampled levels, rather than a complete three-dimensional wind field or pesticide trajectory.
_Avoid_: Drift trajectory when referring to wind observations.
